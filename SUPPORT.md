# Support

## Community support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/zaowu-labs/zaowu/issues) for reproducible defects, usage questions, and feature proposals. Structured forms collect the version, diagnostics, expected outcome, and safety impact.

When reporting a defect, include `zw --version`, `zw doctor --json`, the command shape with secrets removed, expected behavior, and actual error output.

## Security support

Potential vulnerabilities must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and should not be posted publicly before a fix is available.

## Commercial support

Commercial support, policy packs, centralized administration, and team audit capabilities are planned product layers. They are not included or promised by the current open-source CLI release. See [docs/COMMERCIALIZATION.md](docs/COMMERCIALIZATION.md) for the intended boundary.
